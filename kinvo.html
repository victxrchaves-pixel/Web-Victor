Kinvo

Connected home fitness

UX/UI Identity App

Kinvo is a connected fitness ecosystem designed to transform home training into an intelligent, personalized, and immersive experience. The project explores how technology and design can work together to make fitness more accessible, adaptable, and engaging.

The platform integrates workout planning, nutrition management, progress tracking, wearable technology, and smart home devices
into a unified ecosystem. By connecting digital interfaces, equipment, lighting, and music, Kinvo creates a training environment
that responds to each user’s goals, lifestyle, and routines.

The project involved the development of the brand identity,
user experience, user interface, and visual language for both
the digital platform and its connected products. Through a cohesive design approach, Kinvo aims to reduce friction, simplify training,
and make healthy habits easier to build and maintain.
